When operating with a symmetrical supply of ±14V (equivalent to 28V), the circuit ensures an output power of 12W into a 4Ω load and 8W into an 8Ω load according to DIN45500.

The TDA2030 is capable of providing high output current while maintaining extremely low harmonic and crossover distortion.
